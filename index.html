Steps

1. Click on the Download Followers Extention button

2. Click on the Copy Chrome Extensions Link button

3. Paste the Chrome Extensions Link in a new tab

4. In the chrome://extensions/ page on top right turn on developer mode

5. Go back to the Followers Extention you downloaded and unzip it

6. Go back to the chrome://extensions/ page and on top left click on Load unpacked

7. Choose the Followers Extention you just unzipped

8. Go to Roblox home page

9. On top right find the extension icon and click it

10. Find the Roblox Follower and click on that

11. When it pops up click on Copy

12. Go back to the original page

13. Click on the Roblox Followers button

14. Wait a few minutes
